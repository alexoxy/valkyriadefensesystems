About Valkyria

Building the next generation of defense industrial capacity.

Valkyria Defense Systems is a Portugal-based defense industrial initiative focused on developing advanced, large-scale manufacturing capabilities. The initiative brings together industrial expertise, an international technology outlook and global strategic partnerships to support long-term European industrial resilience.

Industrial Development

Choosing the right location for long-term growth.

Valkyria is currently assessing potential industrial locations in Portugal. The selection process prioritizes multimodal logistics and market access, availability of skilled people, dependable energy infrastructure, and opportunities to develop lasting partnerships with the surrounding business and industrial ecosystem.

Portugal's Atlantic position, European connectivity and industrial potential provide a strategic foundation for an initiative designed to serve a wider European defense supply chain.
