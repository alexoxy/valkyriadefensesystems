About Valkyria

Building the next generation of defense industrial capacity.

Valkyria Defense Systems is a Portugal-based industrial initiative focused on establishing advanced defense ammunition manufacturing capabilities. The project is being structured around specialized industrial know-how, technological integration and enduring international cooperation, with an emphasis on quality, reliability and resilient supply chains.

Industrial Development

Choosing the right location for long-term growth.

Valkyria is currently assessing potential industrial locations in Portugal. The selection process prioritizes multimodal logistics and market access, availability of skilled people, dependable energy infrastructure, and opportunities to develop lasting partnerships with the surrounding business and industrial ecosystem.

Portugal's Atlantic position, European connectivity and industrial potential provide a strategic foundation for a project designed to serve a wider European defense supply chain.
